# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Real-estate agents and landlords preparing units for showings or new tenants, and project managers handing over post-construction or post-renovation spaces. Both buy on a deadline, judge the result on whether the space passes a handover inspection without callback, and re-buy when an operator proves predictable. Secondary audience: homeowners with nicer apartments who want it done right and don't want to manage it.

## Product Purpose

Convert handover buyers into bookings by visibly out-classing template cleaning sites in Kragujevac. Every page should read like a premium operator with a documented process, not a contractor with a phone number. Success looks like: the inquiry form fills more often, and prospects arrive on the call already convinced this is the serious option in the market.

## Brand Personality

Pouzdan, precizan, diskretan. Calm operational confidence — the voice of someone who has done this 500 times and won't be late. No exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "100% guaranteed", no "najbolji". Copy speaks in concrete operational facts ("procena u roku od 24h", "predaja bez dorade") rather than emotional claims.

## Anti-references

- Generic template cleaning sites: stock cleaning photos, big green checkmarks, "100% satisfaction" badges, "brzo i jeftino" framing.
- Loud or discount energy: corporate blue, oversaturated CTAs, urgency banners, price-led messaging.
- Bootstrap-card SaaS landing: identical 3- or 4-up icon-and-text card grids, gradient accents on hero metrics, generic "How it works" three-step strips.
- Hero-metric template (big number, small label, supporting stats with gradient accents) — banned in the shared design laws and especially wrong for this brand.
- Side-stripe accent borders, gradient text, decorative glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

1. **Handover-grade, not hospitality-grade.** Every section should answer "is this ready for the next user?" not "does this feel like a spa?". The buyers are operators, not guests; the proof point is callback-free delivery.
2. **Show the work, don't list it.** Proof through photography and before/after evidence, not bullet lists of services. Trust comes from seen results, not described capabilities.
3. **Specifics over superlatives.** Concrete operational facts ("procena u roku od 24h", "predaja bez dorade", "diskretan tim") beat adjectives. Numbers and verbs out-perform claims.
4. **Restraint signals premium.** Fewer claims executed deeper. The amber accent earns its place; never used to shout. Whitespace is not waste — it is the loudest premium signal on the page.
5. **Mobile-first discipline.** The priority buyer often books from a phone during or after a site visit. Mobile is the default surface, not the override; new CSS work is authored mobile-first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Reduced-motion preference respected for entrance animations and the comparison slider. Amber-on-cream and amber-on-dark contrast verified at AA for body text. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile action bar and primary CTAs. Form errors announced via existing `aria-live="polite"` status line. Lightbox already meets dialog semantics (role, focus trap, restore) — keep that bar on any new modal.
